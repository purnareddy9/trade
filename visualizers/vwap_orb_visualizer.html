What is the Nifty 200?

Top 200 Companies

The Nifty 200 represents India’s top 200 largest and most actively traded companies on the NSE. It captures over 85% of India's total market capitalization.

✔ Why it protects intraday traders:
• Millions of shares trade daily (instant fills).
• Tight 5-paise bid/ask spreads (zero slippage).
• Cannot be rigged by small pump-and-dump operators.

What is the F&O List?

~180 Elite Stocks

F&O (Futures & Options) is an elite list of ~180 to 200 stocks approved by SEBI that meet strict liquidity, daily turnover, and institutional participation criteria.

✔ The #1 Superpower of F&O Stocks:
• NO LOWER CIRCUIT LOCKOUTS! F&O stocks do not freeze at 5% lower circuit where you can't sell.
• Institutions (FIIs, DIIs) trade here daily.
• Examples: Reliance, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Birlasoft, Tata Motors.

Exact Setup Guide: Adding VWAP to Your Charts

TradingView Setup
  1. Click Indicators (fx) at the top of your screen.
  2. Type VWAP and select Volume Weighted Average Price.
  3. Hover over VWAP in the top-left and click the Gear Icon (Settings ⚙️).
  4. In Inputs tab: Leave Anchor Period as Session.
  5. In Style tab:
    • Make the VWAP line thick and bright blue.
    • Uncheck Upper Band 1, 2, 3.
    • Uncheck Lower Band 1, 2, 3.
    • Uncheck Bands Fill (removes the shadow).
  6. Click Ok. You now have a clean, institutional VWAP line!
Zerodha Kite Setup
  1. Open any stock chart (e.g. BSOFT, Reliance, Tata Motors).
  2. Change the timeframe from 1D to 5 Minute or 15 Minute.
  3. Click Studies (or fx) at the top of the Kite chart.
  4. Search for VWAP. Click on it.
  5. Click the Settings (Gear Icon ⚙️) on VWAP:
    • Color: Choose Blue or Yellow.
    • Width: Set line width to 2 or 3 for visibility.
  6. Click Done. VWAP automatically starts calculating at 09:15 AM every day!
💡
The Golden VWAP Rule for Intraday: If the stock price is ABOVE VWAP, only look to buy. If the stock price is BELOW VWAP, never buy—institutions are net sellers.
